What is the Cotopaxi Foundation?

The Cotopaxi Foundation is a Utah-based private 501c3 nonprofit organization.

Giving is core to the Cotopaxi model. The foundation is the next step in terms of doing more good. 1% of our annual revenue goes to the Cotopaxi Foundation, which aims to boost our grantmaking, democratize our impact, and help us make a bigger difference in the communities we touch.

Every time you purchase one of our products, you’re contributing to the Cotopaxi Foundation. But we encourage you to be part of Doing Good by giving directly to the foundation. However you support our efforts, your contribution is making a difference.

We are not able to provide tax deductible receipts for our customers at this time. Cotopaxi is exploring how to modify this process in the future, and will post updates here on our website.

Returns & Exchanges

Our Return Policy

We stand behind our products and our Guaranteed for Good™ promise. Our product is durable by design, but if there’s a problem with your gear we’ll do what we can to make things right.

Cotopaxi gladly accepts returns or exchanges within 60 days of purchase of new Cotopaxi products. To receive a refund, please make sure tags are still intact, and that items are unwashed and in excellent condition. You will also need an order number to initiate the return.

A $6 return shipping fee will be deducted from the refund total. For purchases made on cotopaxi.com, you can also make a return at one of our retail stores and avoid the shipping fee. Please be sure to bring your order confirmation or store receipt with you to initiate the return. Check here to find a store in your area.

Return Policy Exceptions

For international and U.S. territory orders, returns are accepted within 60 days of purchase. Customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping back to us. Items marked as "final sale" remain ineligible for returns or exchanges.

Items marked as "final sale" are ineligible for return or exchanges and Event Tickets are non-refundable.

Returns & Exchanges FAQs

Where is my refund?

Once your return has been delivered to us, it typically takes 1-7 days (up to 14 days during busier times) for your refund or store credit to be processed and sent to you. If you opted for store credit, a gift card code will be sent to you via email. If you choose a refund back to the original payment method, it can take up to 10 business days for those funds to show in your account once the refund has been processed.

Can you refund a different credit card?

We aren’t able to refund to a different credit or debit card. If the original card you used to purchase the item is no longer active (e.g., it's a gift card, it’s voided, or it’s expired), we will refund you via a Cotopaxi gift card.

Can I return an international order?

Yes! We accept returns on international orders within 60 days of purchase. Please note that customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping back to us. To initiate a return, reach out to customer support at [email protected] your order number ready.

At this time, we are not able to ship to Afghanistan, Albania, Algeria, Ascension Island, Azerbaijan, Brazil, Caribbean Netherlands, India (Ladakh), Mexico (Oaxaca), Moldova, Morocco, New Zealand (Chatham Islands), Russia, South Sudan, Tristan da Cunha, or Tunisia.

Can I edit my order once it’s placed?

Once an order is placed, we are unable to make any edits or adjustments. If you placed an order in the last 12 hours and want to cancel, please contact us and we will do our best to get that order canceled and refunded, but can’t guarantee it. If we’re unable to cancel it in time and the order ships, you will need to initiate a return at returns.cotopaxi.com.

Do I need to return my free promotional item, too?

If you’re returning items that earned you a free gift with purchase, and the order total minus the value of the returned items still meets the promotion threshold, you can keep the gift! If the total no longer meets the promotion threshold, you will need to return the gift as well. Just make sure you select the promotional item when initiating your return at returns.cotopaxi.com. If you’d like to keep the free gift, we will either deduct the cost of the free item from your refund or charge you for the item.

Can I return my Cotopaxi Más Vida product to a retail store?

Not at this time. Our Cotopaxi Más Vida products are warehoused and fulfilled at a different facility.
