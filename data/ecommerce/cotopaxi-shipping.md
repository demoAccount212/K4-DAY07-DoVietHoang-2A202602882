Global Uprising, PBC dba Cotopaxi ("Cotopaxi") distributes 100% of the monetary donations that it receives from its customers to the Cotopaxi Foundation on a monthly basis.

What is the Cotopaxi Foundation?

The Cotopaxi Foundation is a Utah-based private 501c3 nonprofit organization.

Giving is core to the Cotopaxi model. The foundation is the next step in terms of doing more good. 1% of our annual revenue goes to the Cotopaxi Foundation, which aims to boost our grantmaking, democratize our impact, and help us make a bigger difference in the communities we touch.

Every time you purchase one of our products, you’re contributing to the Cotopaxi Foundation. But we encourage you to be part of Doing Good by giving directly to the foundation. However you support our efforts, your contribution is making a difference.

We are not able to provide tax deductible receipts for our customers at this time. Cotopaxi is exploring how to modify this process in the future, and will post updates here on our website.

Shipping

Shipping

Free Economy Shipping On Orders $99+

To qualify for free shipping, all you have to do is make sure your order is $99 or more before tax.

This offer isn’t valid for international delivery addresses, U.S. territories, P.O. Boxes, or APO/FPO addresses.

Orders ship from Salt Lake City, UT and may vary in transit time based on your distance from Utah.

Shipping Options

Please note, we're not able to ship orders to freight forwarding companies or addresses. This includes any address associated with a freight forwarder, mail consolidator, or third-party reshipping service, even if it's located within a country we normally ship to.
If we identify that an order is destined for a freight forwarding address, we reserve the right to cancel the order and issue a refund. We're unable to reroute or reship orders that have already been sent to one of these addresses.

If an order is not cancelled and is shipped, Cotopaxi is not responsible for any items that are lost, stolen, or missing once they've been delivered to that address. We're also not able to offer refunds or replacements in these cases. Route shipping protection does not apply to freight forwarding addresses.

If you're shopping from outside the countries we currently ship to, please check our list of supported countries here, or our authorized international sites here— we're always working to expand where we deliver.
